Information Collection and Use

Speedometer Universal uses precise and approximate location information to provide its core functionality, including:

  • Real-time GPS speed measurement
  • Distance calculation
  • Trip tracking and trip history
  • Route recording and map display
  • Navigation features
  • Altitude and movement statistics
  • Floating speedometer overlays

Location Information

The application accesses precise and approximate location data through GPS services to calculate speed, distance traveled, routes, and navigation information.

When a user starts a trip, the app may continue accessing location information through a foreground service so that tracking continues while the user uses other applications or turns off the screen. A persistent notification is always displayed while location tracking is active.

If background location permission is granted by the user, location access is used solely to continue an active trip or navigation session initiated by the user. Users can stop tracking at any time from within the application.

Location information and trip history are stored primarily on the user's device. We do not sell, rent, or share location information with third parties for advertising purposes.

Activity Recognition

The app uses the Activity Recognition permission to improve movement detection and enhance walking, running, cycling, and driving distance accuracy. This information is processed locally on the device.

Floating Window Mode

If enabled by the user, Speedometer Universal may display a floating speedometer overlay above other applications. This feature requires the SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW permission and continuously updates speed information during an active trip.
