Information Collection and Use

TV Remote Universal Control requires certain permissions to provide its core features, including:

  • Discovering compatible Smart TVs on your local Wi-Fi network.
  • Connecting and pairing with supported TVs.
  • Sending remote control commands.
  • Voice search on supported TVs.
  • Casting photos, videos, and music.
  • Remembering previously connected devices.

Local Network & Wi-Fi Access

The application scans your local Wi-Fi network to discover compatible Smart TVs. Your phone and TV must be connected to the same Wi-Fi network for remote control functionality.

Information about connected TVs (such as device name or IP address) may be stored locally on your device to enable faster reconnection. This information is never sold or shared for advertising purposes.

Microphone Permission

If you choose to use Voice Search, the application requests microphone permission. Audio is used only while Voice Search is active to send voice commands to your connected TV. We do not record or store your voice.

Photos and Media Access

If you use the Cast feature, the application requests permission to access photos, videos, or audio files stored on your device. These files are accessed only when you choose to cast them to your TV.

Your personal media remains on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.

Nearby Devices

On supported Android versions, the app may request Nearby Devices permission to discover and communicate with compatible Smart TVs and streaming devices on your local network.

Data Storage

The application stores settings such as paired TVs, preferences, themes, and shortcuts locally on your device. This information is not shared with third parties except as required to provide essential app functionality.
